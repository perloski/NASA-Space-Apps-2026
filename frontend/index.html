About SPHEREx

What it is

SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer) is a NASA space observatory designed to map the entire sky in spectrophotometric detail.

How it captures images

Unlike traditional imaging telescopes, SPHEREx captures infrared light across 102 distinct wavelengths. Using linear variable filters, it splits incoming light to obtain high-resolution optical spectrum data for every point on the celestial sphere, creating a comprehensive 3D map of the cosmos.

Why it does it

  • Cosmic Origins: Investigates the physics of cosmic inflation seconds after the Big Bang.
  • Galactic Evolution: Measures light from distant galaxies to trace the history of cosmic star formation.
  • Ingredients for Life: Searches for water ice and organic molecules in interstellar clouds and disk systems across the Milky Way.
